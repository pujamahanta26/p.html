Chole Bhature Recipe

Chole Bhature is a popular North Indian dish. It consists of spicy chickpea curry called chole and soft, fluffy fried bread called bhature.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup boiled chickpeas (chole)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Chole masala
  • Salt and oil as required
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • Curd and water as required

Instructions

  1. Soak chickpeas overnight and boil until soft.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add chopped onions.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes.
  4. Add salt and chole masala, then cook well.
  5. Add boiled chickpeas and simmer the curry.
  6. Prepare bhature dough using flour, curd and water.
  7. Roll the dough and deep-fry until fluffy and golden.
  8. Serve hot chole with bhature.

Tip: Serve with onion and pickle for extra flavour!

Enjoy your delicious Chole Bhature!
